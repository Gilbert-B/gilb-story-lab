# GilB — Production Quality Standard v1.0

**Scope:** Every episode of *Unpacked by GilB* and every subsequent GilB storytelling format.  
**Owner:** GilB  
**Approved benchmark:** GILB-001 — Hearts of Oak (creative reference approved 2026-11-08).  
**Brand:** GilB; `@Ghyl_B`; *There's more to the story.*

## 1. Two separate approvals

- **Creative benchmark approval:** The owner likes the story, design language, editing, pacing, and production direction. GILB-001 has this approval.
- **Publication approval:** The final export meets the factual, legal, accessibility, audio, and technical checks below. Creative approval **does not** imply publication clearance.

## 2. Editorial and research checklist (release blocking)

- [ ] Permanent GILB story ID exists; no duplicate story.
- [ ] Original source and retrieved date recorded with usable URLs, report editions, and relevant data definitions.
- [ ] Original/raw data preserved; cleaning steps reproducible and outputs versioned.
- [ ] Key computed measures independently checked and mathematically reconciled.
- [ ] Comparators use like-for-like periods, populations, units, and denominators.
- [ ] The hook is supported and is not misleading; distinctions between fact, inference, and speculation are clear.
- [ ] Every narrated or on-screen quantitative claim has a verifiable evidence record.
- [ ] No unsupported causal assertions or exaggerated significance.
- [ ] Owner signs off claims in the registry before `Ready to publish`; do not automatically mark evidence as approved.
- [ ] Sources are credited appropriately in the caption, description, or accompanying source notes.

## 3. Narrative and visualisation checklist

- [ ] One central discovery; each new statistic advances the story.
- [ ] Narrative progresses without repeatedly jumping between unrelated measures.
- [ ] Each visual accurately maps to data (e.g. specific matches at specific positions).
- [ ] Graphs have meaningful and explicit benchmarks; bars begin at zero unless the design explains a different baseline.
- [ ] Every win/loss/draw comparison gives an honest denominator.
- [ ] No decorative chart elements imply unearned precision or false comparisons.
- [ ] Mobile preview confirms legibility and adequate viewing time.
- [ ] All transitions have a purpose and do not reveal surprises prematurely.
- [ ] Technical labels and internal IDs are not visible in final public video.
- [ ] Keep subtle `@Ghyl_B` watermark plus GilB outro without obscuring data.

## 4. Audio checklist

- [ ] Owner narration sounds natural and recognisable; do not imitate another person's voice.
- [ ] Clean noise conservatively; no audible pumping, metallic speech, or hard edit artifacts.
- [ ] Music can be heard on a phone speaker but speech is intelligible throughout.
- [ ] Sound-effect peaks do not obscure figures or spoken words.
- [ ] No clipping; review true peak and loudness for target platform (a practical starting point is near -16 to -14 LUFS integrated, not a universal mandate).
- [ ] Narration and key visual reveals synchronised; review on both headphones and phone speaker.
- [ ] Music and sound effects are original or their reuse license is documented.

## 5. Technical/accessibility checklist (release blocking)

- [ ] Native vertical master at **1080 × 1920 (9:16)** or higher is exported from the actual source project (not merely upscaled from a low-resolution file).
- [ ] H.264/AAC MP4 is playable; frame rate and audio duration verified.
- [ ] Captions match the recorded speech and avoid platform UI safe-zone overlays.
- [ ] Text and numerals are readable on a mobile screen; no essential details are behind badges/captions.
- [ ] Export watched start-to-finish on a phone, including first two seconds and branded outro.
- [ ] Correct file version and integrity checksum recorded; editable project files preserved separately.
- [ ] Potential rights/permissions for third-party crests, imagery, footage, audio and data reuse reviewed.

## 6. Publishing and post-publication

- [ ] Owner explicitly approves *release version*, not just draft visuals.
- [ ] Registry is set to `Ready to publish` only after all evidence claims are explicitly approved.
- [ ] Publish platform links, publication dates, and captions saved after posting; never pre-mark as `Published`.
- [ ] Monitor retention, average watch time, shares, saves, comments and audience response.
- [ ] Record lessons learned and suggestions for the next episode.

## Release decision

Mark each check Pass / Needs work / Not applicable. The final release record must identify reviewer, date, export filename, SHA-256, supporting evidence, and any approved exceptions. **Any unresolved release-blocking item means the story remains in Review.**
