# GILB-002 — Mobile Internet Market: Analysis Findings v1.0

**Story ID:** GILB-002  
**Stage:** Analysis completed; editorial selection and publication verification pending  
**Analysed on:** 2026-11-08  
**Data period:** January 2024 — July 2026 (31 month-end observations, 3 operators, 93 operator-month records)  
**Primary source:** National Communications Authority (NCA), official monthly mobile network data operator subscription counts.

## Executive finding

Ghana's mobile-network internet subscriptions increased from **23,247,115** in January 2024 to **30,800,194** in July 2026 — **32.49% growth**.

Although MTN increased its count by **5,673,061**, its **subscription market share declined by 1.60 percentage points**, from **81.61%** to **80.02%**. This is not an uninterrupted decline: MTN fell as low as **77.75% in June 2025** and recovered to about 80% by July 2026.

Telecel's subscriptions grew **61.88%**, compared with **29.90%** for MTN; Telecel's share increased **2.92 percentage points**. AT's July 2026 share was **3.88%**, compared with a peak of **6.81% in May 2024**.

**Working editorial insight:** A leading operator can gain millions of subscriptions yet lose market share when another operator expands faster *in percentage terms*.

## Unusual monthly changes — follow-up research (not explanations)

- **Telecel, June 2025:** +385,538 subscriptions (+9.52% month-on-month). 
- **Telecel, August 2025:** −279,842 (−6.20%); September 2025 +283,016 (+6.68%). 
- **AT, December 2025:** −173,037 (−11.73%).
- **MTN, January 2026:** −527,881 (−2.31%), followed by February and March recoveries.

These movements are published in NCA figures, but their causes are **not established by this dataset**. Follow up with NCA bulletins, operator statements, reporting-methodology changes, tariff/coverage announcements, and other independently corroborated sources before any causal claim.

## Data-quality and interpretation rules

- 31 monthly totals reconcile exactly with the sum of MTN, Telecel, and AT in the supplied dataset.
- Selected endpoint and notable movement figures were spot-checked against NCA published PDFs; complete row-level source transcription should undergo a second independent check before publication.
- The NCA page currently lists a January–July 2026 report as its latest monthly mobile-internet download.
- The NCA 2023 'mobile data' link appears misdirected to a voice report; 2023 **excluded** until corrected.
- **Mobile internet subscriptions != unique subscribers or people.** One person may hold multiple SIMs.
- Counts measure the number of active mobile-network data subscriptions (MNOs), not mobile voice, fixed broadband, BWA, mobile data traffic, revenue, or mobile money.
- Market shares in this workbook use **MTN + Telecel + AT mobile-network data subscriptions** as the denominator, never the sum of fixed and mobile subscriptions.
- Growth contribution percentages measure a proportion of **net stock changes**, not the share of newly recruited users.
- Percent **change** and percentage-point **change in share** must not be confused.
- Don't imply shifts from one operator to another unless supported by customer-porting, churn, or equivalent data.
