# GILB-001 — Animation Production Blueprint v1.0

> **Status:** Draft for owner review; the interactive storyboard and narration v1.0 are approved for design, but individual factual claims are NOT approved for publication.
> **Creator:** GilB · **Series:** Unpacked by GilB · **Story:** Hearts of Oak, Ghana Premier League 2025/26
> **Source of truth:** `stories/GILB-001/record.json`; this blueprint is a production asset, not a replacement for the registry.

## 1. Production brief

- **Deliverable:** 9:16 vertical motion-graphics explainer, eventually MP4 (1080×1920, 30 fps).
- **Narration:** Approved v1.0 verbatim; do not rewrite or rearrange beats.
- **Working duration:** 1:44 (104 seconds). These are provisional storyboard timings, **not** final timed voiceover.
- **Format and platforms:** 9:16 for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts; one main master.
- **Design approach:** Clean editorial motion graphics based on data, no generic football poster imagery.
- **Signature reveal:** exactly 13 of the 24 clean-sheet tiles turn into 0–0 outcomes while the visual identity of the grid persists.
- **End card:** “Unpacked by GilB” and “There’s more to the story.”

## 2. Locked constraints and creative system

| Element | Specification |

| Captions | Accurate, human-reviewed subtitles; avoid bottom and right-side social UI regions |
| Audio | Voiceover first. Original or licensed bed and restrained effects. Music must never compete with narration |

**Important:** Counts are grouped by match outcome, **not** ordered chronologically. Do not invent fixture timelines or visual claims of match order.

## 3. Scene-by-scene animation blueprint

Every scene below is tied to the previously approved 14-scene interactive prototype. Times are temporary and must be retimed against a natural voice recording.

### Scene 01 — Hook / mystery (00:00–00:08, 8s)

**Approved voiceover (verbatim scene excerpt):**

> What if I told you a Ghana Premier League club kept 24 clean sheets in 34 matches... and still didn’t win the title?

**Visible text:** 24 clean sheets. No title.

**Composition:** Single dominant 24, small /34; club name withheld; dark negative space.

**Motion beat plan:** 0.0–1.0s: empty midnight canvas → quiet 24; 1.0–3.2s: 34 matches appears; 3.2–6.0s: clean sheets locks underneath; 6.0–8.0s: NO TITLE replaces the lower label.

**Transition:** At final beat, collapse the 24 statistic to upper position and resolve to Hearts wordmark on next shot.

**Sound:** Low pulse on first 24; dry impact on NO TITLE. No exaggerated stadium sounds.

**Evidence / analysis:** EV-001; EV-002

**Acceptance check:** Do not reveal Hearts until the next scene; question remains legible 1.5s.

**Visible text:** HEARTS OF OAK · 15 CONCEDED

**Composition:** Typographic Hearts name over muted team strip; oversized 15 and precise label goals conceded.

**Motion beat plan:** 0.0–1.2s: Hearts name enters; 1.2–3.8s: numeric count to 15 with 15 as endpoint; 3.8–7.0s: hold + subtle rule growth.

**Transition:** Transform oversized 15 into a small defensive context tag as a 34-match grid enters.

**Sound:** Subtle count clicks, not 15 distinct loud hits.

**Evidence / analysis:** EV-001; EV-002; needs claim mapping for 15

### Scene 03 — Defensive proof (00:15–00:21, 6s)

**Approved voiceover (verbatim scene excerpt):**

> They kept an incredible 24 clean sheets.

**Visible text:** 24 CLEAN SHEETS / 34 MATCHES

**Composition:** Persistent 34-match 6-column tile grid. 24 amber clean-sheet states, 10 neutral non-clean-sheet states.

**Sound:** 24 soft grouped ticks, one single settling tone.

**Sound:** One understated transition sweep; drop music under 13 for emphasis.

**Evidence / analysis:** EV-004

**Acceptance check:** Exactly 13 teal 0–0, 11 amber other clean sheets, 10 neutral; label makes subset clear.

## 4. Technical implementation approach

1. **Keep the existing browser storyboard as the visual reference.** Do not replace it with AI-generated imagery. Use its scene layouts, typography and persistent tile-grid idea.
2. Build a reusable **scene renderer** for 1080×1920 output (HTML/CSS/SVG + JavaScript or another renderer with equivalent fidelity). Scenes should be time-addressable so any timestamp produces a deterministic frame.
3. Define **data-driven graphic components**: a 34-tile match matrix, a count-up display, two-value comparison bars with zero baselines, a two-team W/D/L/points comparison, and a GilB end card.
4. Ensure transitions **preserve tile identity**. Scene 03→04 should not cut to a new layout. Scene 09 uses the same underlying match dataset for 18 scoreless fixtures.
5. Obtain/record the actual narration before fixing timestamps. Align each voiceover phrase with its visual action and hold the big reveal long enough to read.
6. Add accurate captions and original/rights-cleared sound effects. Retain a licensing/provenance note for every external asset.
7. Render short proof clips first (especially Scenes 03–05), then a full draft with audio. No automatic publishing.
8. Recalculate/verify statistics before moving the story to final Review or Ready to publish.
