## Motion direction

1. **Visual grammar:** A reveal, not a slideshow. Each graphic must physically transform into the next idea where possible: 24 tiles → 13 zero-zero tiles; defence shield → attack; wins → league points.
2. **Typography:** Large headline numbers; short overlays (ideally fewer than 7 words); sentence-level captions timed to narration, not paragraphs.
3. **GilB palette:** Midnight `#101C2B`, Ivory `#F3F0E8`, Amber `#D9AB56`, Teal `#36A7A3`; restrained football club accent colours only where appropriately licensed.
4. **Mobile safety:** Keep main titles, labels, counts and key diagrams inside a conservative central region of the 9:16 frame (approximately x=90–990 and y=220–1580), allowing TikTok/Instagram UI and caption overlays to vary.
5. **Readability:** Do not show four statistics at once. Each new number stays long enough to register; minimum ~1.5s hold on the 13-goalless-draw reveal and ~1s on 8-point gap.
6. **Source attribution:** Small but legible attribution in closing or pinned caption: GFA season summary, GFA final league table and RSSSF match-results record; include calculation notes in post description as appropriate.
7. **Editorial safety:** Match results explain numerical points outcomes; they do not establish tactics, player intent, goalkeeper quality or alternative counterfactual outcomes.
8. **Audio:** Use narration-led timing with restrained sounds on tile flips and key statistic reveals. Do not use unlicensed commercial music.
9. **Visual concept sheet:** An exploratory contact-sheet rendering may help communicate mood, but **any text, logos, layout, duration or numbers in generated art must be replaced or checked** against this document. The deliverable is a vertical video, not a landscape contact sheet.
