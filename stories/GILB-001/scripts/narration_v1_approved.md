What if I told you a Ghana Premier League club kept **24 clean sheets in 34 matches**... and still didn't win the title?

In the 2025/26 season, Hearts of Oak conceded just **15 goals** throughout the entire league campaign.

They kept an incredible **24 clean sheets**.

But here's the surprising part.

**13 of those clean sheets ended in goalless draws.**

Thirteen matches where their opponents couldn't score... but neither could Hearts.

And that's the problem. **Football isn't just about stopping goals. You also have to score them.**

Hearts managed only **25 goals in 34 matches**.

That's an average of just **0.74 goals per game**, compared to the league average of **1.02**.

In fact, they failed to score in **18 matches**.

Now, compare that with the eventual champions, Medeama.

Both clubs lost exactly **six matches**.

But while Medeama won **17 games**, Hearts managed just **13**.

And that difference in wins and draws left Hearts **eight points behind the champions**.

Hearts had an extraordinary defence. But they couldn't turn enough of those clean sheets into victories.
