## Results

| Claim in the video | Check | Result |
|---|---|---|
| About 23 in 100 cedis of 2025 output from agriculture | GSS Table 1.2: agriculture **22.78%** of GDP at basic prices, **current prices** (nominal), 2025 provisional. At constant 2013 prices it would be 22.0% (Table 1.3: 44,013 / 200,104). | **Verified.** Settles the real-vs-nominal question: 22.8% is the nominal share. Graphic Online (18 Mar 2026) wrongly calls it "of real GDP". |
| Agriculture grew 6.8% in 2025 | GSS Table 1.4: **6.78%** | **Verified** |
| Economy grew 6% | GSS Table 1.4: **5.95%** (GDP at purchasers' value); GSS homepage shows 6.0% | **Verified** |
| Just over 4 in 100 cedis of bank loans in agriculture, end-2025 | BoG Table A.5 (data table, GH¢m): agriculture **4,805.21** of **110,972.72** = **4.33%** | **Verified** in a data table (no longer chart-only) |
| Share fell from 4.7% to 4.3% | Table A.5: Dec 2024 **4,495.79 / 95,540.33 = 4.71%**; Dec 2025 **4.33%**. Figure 6.1 labels (4.7, 4.3) agree. | **Verified** |
| Bank loans to agriculture went up | Table A.5: +GH¢309.42m, **+6.88%** | **Verified** (the research pack's estimate was ≈ +6%; the actual figure is +6.9%) |
| Bank loans overall went up faster | Table A.5 grand total **+16.15%**; MPR text: "increased by 16.2 percent … to GH¢111.0 billion" | **Verified** |
| "These are bank loans, not every source of financing" | Table A.5 is "DMB's Credit Allocations" (deposit money banks) | **Verified** (scope note) |

The 4.5% figure in the press is agriculture's share of **private-sector** credit: 4,805.21 / 106,189.69 = 4.53%. This confirms the denominator note in the research pack.
