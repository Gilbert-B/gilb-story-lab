## Sources and claims

All figures, sources and computations are in [`../../evidence/GILB006_Research_Pack.md`](../../evidence/GILB006_Research_Pack.md): the claim table is §3, the computations §4 and the sources §10. The data table is [`../../data/clean/GILB006_Data.csv`](../../data/clean/GILB006_Data.csv).

- **Jobs:** GSS Quarterly Labour Force Survey, Q1–Q3 2025. Agriculture is 37.2% of employment, about 5.0 million people.
- **Output and growth:** GSS 2025 annual GDP release (17 Mar 2026), as reported by GNA. Agriculture is 22.8% of output; it grew 6.8%, against 6.0% for the whole economy.
- **Bank loans:** Bank of Ghana, Monetary Policy Report, January 2026, Figure 6.1.
  - Agriculture's share of total bank credit outstanding was 4.7% in Dec 2024 and 4.3% in Dec 2025.
  - Gross loans were GH¢111.0bn in Dec 2025, up 16.2%.
